# Competitive Research & UX Benchmarks: Crust & Q

This document benchmarks four premier US restaurant ordering and food delivery applications (DoorDash, Uber Eats, Domino's, Sweetgreen) to extract validated interaction patterns, identify user friction, and establish Crust & Q's distinct market positioning.

---

## 1. Competitive Benchmarking Matrix

| Competitor / Product | Key UX Strengths | UX Weaknesses & Frictions | Actionable Solution for Crust & Q |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **DoorDash** | • Fast category pills and highly visible dynamic ETA pills.<br>• Seamless "DoubleDash" cart add-on recommendations.<br>• Real-time map tracking with multi-stage progress bars. | • Visual clutter with aggressive ad banners and sponsored rows.<br>• Hidden "service fees" revealed late in checkout.<br>• Disconnected driver chat experience during delays. | • Maintain clean light-theme hierarchy with zero third-party ads.<br>• Itemize delivery and service fees transparently on both Cart and Checkout.<br>• Embed 1-tap call/message directly on the live tracking map HUD. |

## 2. Key UX Differentiators & Product Positioning

1. **Artisanal Food Identity, Not a Tech Utility**:
   Unlike generic third-party aggregator grids filled with logos, Crust & Q presents its wood-fired pizzas and slow-smoked craft BBQ on 3D porcelain plates with warm editorial lighting and rich sensory descriptions.
2. **Transparent, Reconciled Pricing**:
   All promotions (e.g. `CRUST50`), delivery fees, taxes, and driver tips calculate with mathematical consistency from the cart drawer through the final digital receipt.
3. **Bi-Directional Real-Time Cartography**:
   Both the customer and delivery partner share synchronized daylight Google Maps tracking centered on Downtown Orlando and Lake Eola, eliminating delivery confusion.
4. **Focused Ergonomics**:
   A streamlined 2-tab floating dock (**Delivery** and **History**) keeps core workflows immediately reachable within the natural thumb zone.
