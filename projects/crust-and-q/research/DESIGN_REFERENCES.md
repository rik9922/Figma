# Design References & Visual Direction: Crust & Q

This document defines the sensory visual language, lighting characteristics, materiality, and component styling for Crust & Q's mobile ecosystem.

---

## 1. Aesthetic Pillars & Materiality

- **Visual Style**: **Warm Casual Dining Light Theme**. Combining editorial food studio photography with modern American bistro typography.
- **Lighting & Ambiance**: Daylight, sunlit warmth, and natural wood tones. Clean, crisp, and high-contrast surfaces without muddy dark gradients or night-mode dimming.
- **Surface Layering**:
  - **Ground**: Warm Cream (`#FFF8F0`) providing a welcoming, non-sterile canvas.
  - **Card Surfaces**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with 14–18px rounded radii and soft 8pt elevation shadows (`rgba(41, 40, 36, 0.06)`).
  - **Borders & Dividers**: Warm Oatmeal Hairlines (`#EFE8DE`) preventing stark grey borders.
- **Photography Treatment**:
  - Studio top-down and 45-degree angle dishes plated on porcelain white ceramic.
  - Authentic wood-fired blistered crusts, melted cheese glazes, and Texas oak bark textures.

---

## 2. Color System Strategy & Palette Mapping

- **Primary Anchor**: **Tomato Flame (`#D93B18`)**
  - Represents fire, wood ovens, and appetite. Used for primary CTAs, active indicators, and category highlights.
- **Secondary Anchor**: **Fresh Herb Green (`#3F795F`)**
  - Represents fresh basil, oregano, and quality ingredients. Used for rating badges, positive statuses, and fast-delivery tags.
- **Accent Anchor**: **Golden Crust (`#D97706`)**
  - Represents baked golden crusts and slow-smoked BBQ glazes. Used for Pitmaster picks and reward highlights.
- **Typography Neutrals**:
  - **Primary Text**: Warm Charcoal (`#292824`) — delivers a verified **14.01:1** contrast ratio on white/cream grounds.
  - **Secondary Text**: Warm Stone (`#57534E`) — delivers **7.24:1** contrast.
  - **Muted Text**: Warm Taupe (`#8C827A`) — used for subtle captions.

---

## 3. Component & Ergonomics Standards

- **Touch Ergonomics**: All interactive buttons, chips, and dock items meet a minimum touch target of **48 × 48 pt**.
- **Icons**: 100% vector SVG paths from the official Lucide suite. Zero unicode emojis.
- **Navigation Dock**: Floating pill dock (`#1C1B19`) anchored at `y=754` with a dark contrast shell, 29px corner radius, and 16px soft shadow.
