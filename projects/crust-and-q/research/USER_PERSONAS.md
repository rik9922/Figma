# Target User Personas: Crust & Q

This document details verified user personas, behavioral drivers, daily contexts, and critical needs for both the Customer and Delivery Partner experiences of Crust & Q.

---

## Persona 1: Sarah Jenkins — The Tech-Forward Foodie (Customer Role)

- **Demographics**: 28 years old, Senior Product Marketing Manager, lives in Lake Eola Heights, Orlando, FL.
- **Tech Stack**: iPhone 15 Pro, Apple Pay, everyday mobile delivery user.
- **Context & Habits**:
  - Orders takeout 2–3 times a week after late office meetings or gym workouts.
  - Prioritizes artisanal quality over generic fast food, but refuses to wait 60+ minutes.
  - Frequently customizes pizza toppings (extra basil, light cheese, gluten-friendly options for friends).
- **Core Goals**:
  1. Build a custom artisan pizza or order craft smoked BBQ in under 2 minutes without confusing option menus.
  2. Know the exact ETA and watch the driver's vehicle on live GPS so she can buzz them up to her apartment lobby without delay.
  3. Earn Pitmaster Points on every order to unlock free sides and weekend meal discounts.
- **Current Pain Points**:
  - Aggregator apps charge $8+ in hidden "service fees" and delivery surcharges.
  - Soggiest crust arrives because drivers take multi-order batched routes.
  - Inaccurate tracking that says "Driver arriving in 5 mins" for 20 minutes straight.
- **Key UX Requirements for Crust & Q**:
  - Clear 7-step customizer with visual confirmation of selected crust and toppings.
  - Daylight Google Maps view with real street avenues, driver pulse marker, and verified countdown ETA.
  - Instant Apple Pay 1-tap checkout.

---

## Persona 2: Marcus Vance — The Experienced Courier (Delivery Partner Role)

- **Demographics**: 32 years old, Full-Time Courier, drives a silver 2021 Honda Civic, Orlando metropolitan area.
- **Tech Stack**: Google Pixel 8 / iPhone 13, Bluetooth mounted phone cradle, Google Maps Navigation.
- **Context & Habits**:
  - Works 30–35 hours a week across lunch and dinner peak demand hours (11:30 AM – 2:00 PM, 5:30 PM – 9:30 PM).
  - Averages 18–22 deliveries a day. Highly focused on maximizing earnings per active hour.
- **Core Goals**:
  1. See the exact guaranteed payout (fare + customer tip) and delivery route *before* accepting a dispatch offer.
  2. Complete pickups quickly without waiting in long restaurant lines.
  3. Seamlessly capture photo proof of delivery at customer doorsteps to prevent delivery disputes.
- **Current Pain Points**:
  - Platforms masking customer tips or reducing base pay when tips are high.
  - Tiny 5-second offer buttons that expire while driving through traffic.
  - Missing gate codes or complex apartment delivery notes leading to wasted time.
- **Key UX Requirements for Crust & Q**:
  - Prominent 15-second radial countdown offer displaying guaranteed fare + tip breakdown.
  - Clean turn-by-turn navigation HUD with customer delivery notes ("Leave at door, don't ring bell").
  - Rapid doorstep photo proof viewfinder with GPS confirmation tag and instant trip credit.
